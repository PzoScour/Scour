Privacy Policy

What Scour collects, why, and the tracking that comes with our affiliate links.

Effective: July 26, 2026

1. Overview

Scour ("Scour," "we," "us") is a price-comparison tool for automotive parts. This policy explains what information Scour collects when you use Scour, and how that information is used. Scour does not currently have user accounts, logins, or checkout — the data footprint today is deliberately small.

2. Information We Collect

Today, Scour collects:

  • Search inputs — the vehicle year, make, model, part name, and condition preference you enter into the search form, used to query retailer/marketplace APIs on your behalf.
  • Standard technical data collected automatically by any web server or hosting provider — things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, used for basic operation, security, and abuse prevention.

We do not currently require or collect an account, name, mailing address, or payment information — Scour doesn't process payments, since all purchases happen on the retailer's own site.

3. How We Use Information

Search inputs are used to run the search you asked for and are not sold or used for purposes unrelated to operating and improving Scour. Technical data is used to keep the site running reliably and securely.

4. Affiliate Links & Tracking Cookies

Scour earns commission through affiliate partner networks, currently including eBay Partner Network, CJ Affiliate, Impact.com, and Walmart's affiliate program. When you click a link to a retailer, that network may set a tracking cookie or use a similar technique in your browser to attribute a resulting purchase back to Scour. This is how the retailer knows to pay us a commission — it does not give Scour access to your payment details or personal account information at the retailer.

These cookies are set and controlled by the affiliate networks and retailers, not by Scour directly. You can control or clear them through your browser's cookie and privacy settings; doing so may affect whether a purchase is attributed to Scour but will not affect your ability to shop at the retailer.

5. Third-Party Websites

Scour links out to third-party retailers and marketplaces. Once you leave Scour, that site's own privacy policy applies to any information you provide there. Scour is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites.

6. Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell personal information. We share limited technical data with the third-party services that make Scour work — the retailer/marketplace APIs we query (e.g., eBay), affiliate networks (for commission attribution as described above), and infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics) — solely to operate the site.

7. Data Retention

Because Scour does not currently store accounts or order history, search inputs are used to serve the immediate request and are not retained in a form tied to you beyond standard, short-lived server logs kept for operational and security purposes.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you'd like to ask what information (if any) Scour holds related to you, or have a privacy question, contact us at Luke.p2009@icloud.com and we'll respond to the extent applicable given the minimal data Scour currently collects.

9. Children's Privacy

Scour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Scour adds features (such as accounts) that change what data is collected. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Luke.p2009@icloud.com.
