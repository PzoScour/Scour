Affiliate Disclosure

How Scour makes money, in plain terms.

Effective: July 26, 2026

Scour is free to use. It stays that way because Scour participates in affiliate marketing programs, including the eBay Partner Network, CJ Affiliate, Impact.com, and Walmart's affiliate program.

In plain terms: when you click a "View at [Retailer]" link on Scour and go on to make a qualifying purchase, Scour may earn a commission from that retailer — at no additional cost to you. The price you pay is the same whether you find the item through Scour or go directly to the retailer's site.

These commissions are how Scour is funded. They do not influence which results are shown or how they're ranked — Scour's price and value comparisons are based on the listing data itself (price, condition, seller rating, shipping), not on which retailer pays the highest commission.

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the FTC's guidelines concerning the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising, and applies to every outbound retailer link on Scour.
